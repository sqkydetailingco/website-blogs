When we arrived in Glen Iris, the McLaren GT was dusty. A dull film sat over the dark grey paint, the black wheels and the glass, and dust like that hides what a car really looks like.

As with every job, we started by looking the car over so we knew exactly what we were working with. A Reset Clean covers the exterior and the interior in one visit, and on this McLaren that took 3.5 hours on site.

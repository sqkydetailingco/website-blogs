A corner car wash is built for volume. The result depends on the day, the queue and whatever happened to the car before yours. That is fine for a daily runner. It is a poor fit for a McLaren, where an owner notices every missed edge and every patch of dust left in a shut line.

The owner told us the results from their corner car wash had been inconsistent. Never terrible every time, just never the same twice, and never something they could rely on. What they wanted was predictability: the same standard of clean on every visit, without driving the car anywhere.

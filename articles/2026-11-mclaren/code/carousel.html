Exterior Wash & Dry

The exterior was washed and dried carefully to lift off the dust and road film sitting on the paint. It is the starting point for every Reset Clean.

Door Jambs & Sills

With the dihedral door up, the jamb and sill get cleaned too, the part owners see every time they get in. A ceramic spray topper can add around three months of protection on top.

Interior & Exterior, One Visit

A Reset Clean covers the cabin and the outside in a single booking, so there are no separate trips and nothing is left half done.

Wheels & Calipers

Wheel faces, spokes and the orange calipers were cleaned of brake dust and road film. It builds up fast, which is where a maintenance plan earns its place.

Wheel Arches

The arch was cleaned along with the wheel face, so the area around the wheel matches the paint instead of sitting dusty and dull.

Paint Gloss

Dust flattens dark paint. With it gone, the gloss comes back. A Reset Clean cleans rather than corrects, so for swirl marks we would add an Enhancement Polish or Full Paint Correction.

Intakes & Panel Gaps

Side intakes and panel lines collect dust that a quick wash skips. Cleaned, the bodywork reads clean along the whole side of the car.

Badge & Bonnet

Dust settles hardest around badges and edges. Cleaned, the bonnet gives a clear reflection of the lights above.

Rear Badge & Diffuser

Chrome and tight rear corners need slow, careful work. The badge now sits on clean paint instead of in a ring of dust.

Roof & Rear Deck

From above you see every surface at once: roof, glass and rear deck. All of it was cleaned, so nothing looks missed from any angle.

Exhaust & Rear Quarter

The round exhaust tip and rear quarter were cleaned together. If the metal needs more, exhaust polishing can be added to a visit.

Lights & Lenses

Clean lenses and clean paint around them keep the rear of the car looking sharp, day or night.

Rear Glass & Finish

The last pass over the rear glass, exhaust and bodywork makes sure there is no dust left sitting in the lines.

Cockpit Clean

Leather, trim, screen and steering wheel were cleaned, so the cream and black cabin feels fresh the moment the door opens.

Seats & Leather

The driver's seat and sill take the most wear. A leather balm treatment can be added to keep the leather conditioned between cleans.

Console & Controls

Controls and console surfaces were cleaned, including the gaps around buttons where dust collects and a quick wipe does not reach.

Door Panels & Speakers

Dust in small details like speaker grilles and panel edges is what makes a cabin look uncared for. Our Concierge Care plans keep it at this standard between visits.

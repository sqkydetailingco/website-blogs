Alt: Side-by-side diagram of paintwork under a microscope. On the uncoated side, pollutants settle into the tiny pores of the paintwork. On the coated side, a smooth ceramic coat sits over the paintwork and pollutants glide across the surface.

### new-car-paint-system-layers-ceramic-coat-diagram.png (1080x1440)
Alt: Diagram of a car's paint system built from four stacked layers: primer at the base, colour above it, a clear coat over the colour, and a ceramic coat as the outermost protective layer.

### nxtzen-coating-performance-maintained-vs-irregular-care-graph.png (1080x1080)
Alt: Line graph of coating performance over five years since application. A coating on a regular maintenance plan holds strong performance through the 5-year protection guarantee, while a coating with irregular care declines steadily and ends far lower.
